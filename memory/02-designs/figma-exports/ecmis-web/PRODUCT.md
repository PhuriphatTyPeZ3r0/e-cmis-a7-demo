# Product

## Register

product

## Users

เจ้าหน้าที่สำนักงาน ป.ป.ท. (PACC officers) working at government-issued workstations during office hours. They investigate corruption cases, manage complaint files, track case progress through legal stages, and generate statutory reports. Most are mid-career civil servants familiar with Thai government systems; some are senior investigators managing complex multi-year cases.

## Product Purpose

E-CMIS (Electronic Case Management Intelligence System) is the official digital platform for สำนักงานคณะกรรมการป้องกันและปราบปรามการทุจริตในภาครัฐ (สำนักงาน ป.ป.ท.) to manage corruption complaint investigations end-to-end: receiving complaints across 15 channels, conducting investigations, tracking case files through inquiry, committee decisions, arrest warrants, court processes, and producing statistical reports. Success means every case is traceable, every action is auditable, and no file falls through the cracks.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, trustworthy. The system carries institutional weight: it is the official record of the state. Tone is formal but not hostile. Dense information is expected; operators are trained professionals, not casual users.

## Anti-references

- Old-style Thai government portals: clip-art icons, Comic Sans, rainbow-flag borders, garish banner images
- Corporate ERP gray-on-gray (SAP, Oracle): monochrome bureaucratic forms that feel like 2005
- Dark cyberpunk / hacker aesthetic: matrix green, neon on black, terminal-style — inappropriate for a serious legal institution
- Consumer SaaS softness (Notion, Slack): pill buttons, pastel gradients, playful copy

## Design Principles

1. **Institutional weight without bureaucratic rot.** The system must feel official and authoritative, not antiquated or hostile.
2. **Density earns trust.** Officers manage complex cases; information density is a feature, not a problem to be smoothed away.
3. **Every state is visible.** Case status, deadlines, outstanding actions, and role-specific access must be unambiguous at a glance.
4. **Thai language first.** All UI copy, labels, and error messages default to Thai. Layout must accommodate Thai text reflow.
5. **Predictable over surprising.** Consistency across modules reduces cognitive load for staff rotating across case types.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target specified. Design for strong color contrast (aim for AA ratios), clear focus states, and legible type at standard monitor distances. Support Thai-language screen readers where possible.
